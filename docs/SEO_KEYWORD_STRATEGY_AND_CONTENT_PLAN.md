## 7. Image SEO Guidelines

- Modern WebP format with PNG/SVG fallbacks.
- Explicit `width` and `height` dimensions to prevent Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS).
- High priority eager loading on the LCP logo and hero banner; `loading="lazy"` on all below-the-fold media.
- Descriptive alt attributes accurately reflecting the visible automotive service without keyword stuffing.
