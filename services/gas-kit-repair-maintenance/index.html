Frequently Asked Questions

How frequently should a CNG kit be serviced?

We recommend routine maintenance every 10,000 km or 12 months, including gas filter replacement, pressure checking, and leak inspections.

What are the symptoms of a failing gas kit component?

Common indicators include engine jerking on acceleration, unexpected switching back to petrol, rough idling on gas, or faint odor of gas.
