Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a sequential CNG kit installation take?

A standard sequential CNG kit installation is typically completed in 6 to 8 hours by our specialized mechanical and electrical team.

Will CNG kit installation damage my vehicle engine?

No. Modern sequential injection systems inject gas precisely timed with engine valve openings, preventing backfiring, overheating, and preserving engine life.
